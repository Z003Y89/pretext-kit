pretext-kit examples

Accuracy

Every helper is swept against the painted DOM in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox, at Playwright deviceScaleFactor 1, 1.25 and 2, over Latin, CJK, Arabic, emoji chat, URLs, soft-hyphenated German and French, and file paths, in four named macOS fonts. Each cell is one browser, zoom factor, helper and corpus, coloured by the worst outcome in it. Click a cell for sample cases.

pass: the kit agreed with Pretext and the browser painted it pretext-gap: Pretext itself differs from the browser there, so the kit can't be judged platform: a proven browser painting quirk (WebKit 26 floors line heights) kit-mismatch: the kit wrong where Pretext was right

Reading the grid

  • The judgement and every outcome are defined in verify/RESULTS.md; these cells are exported from verify/results/latest.json.gz by examples/build-accuracy-data.ts, which checks its totals against RESULTS.md.
  • Zoom factors 1.25 and 2 sweep every fourth width, so their cells hold about a quarter of the cases.
  • Zoom here is deviceScaleFactor emulation on macOS: it shows a finer device grid changes nothing, not how Windows or Linux measure.
